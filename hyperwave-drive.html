01 / OVERVIEW

Project Overview

Hyperwave Drive was created during the Mashup Game Jam 2025. The project was built around combining its core gameplay ideas into a cohesive experience while working within the limited timeframe of a game jam.

My main focus throughout development was turning the initial concept into a playable game while balancing gameplay, presentation, and the overall player experience.

The project gave me an opportunity to experiment with game mechanics, visual presentation, and audio while rapidly iterating toward a finished build.

02 / DEVELOPMENT

Development Process

01

DAY 1

I focused on establishing the game's overall aesthetic. I researched vaporwave-inspired visuals and created several sketches to explore the visual direction of Hyperwave Drive.

02

DAY 2

I created a simple 2D prototype in Unity to establish the basic gameplay. I also implemented object pooling to efficiently handle objects being repeatedly created and destroyed.

03

DAY 3

I created the game's music track and began developing its visual effects. Shaders were added to make the visuals more vibrant and help push the vaporwave aesthetic.

04

DAY 4

I expanded the core gameplay by implementing the shooting system. A LineRenderer was used to create the visual effect for the player's shots.

05

DAY 5

I designed and created the enemy sprites. This helped establish more personality within the game's visual style while giving the different enemies their own appearance.

06

DAY 6

I continued building out the game's aesthetics by adding effects such as aftertrails. These effects helped emphasize movement and made the game's visuals feel more energetic.

07

DAY 7

The final day focused on polishing the game and creating the menus. I refined the overall presentation and prepared the final build for submission.

03 / VISUAL DEVELOPMENT

Visual Development

The visual direction for Hyperwave Drive was built around a limited color palette, using a small selection of bold colors to create a consistent retro look throughout the game.

I drew inspiration from retro games and vaporwave aesthetics, combining pixel-based visuals, neon colors, and stylized effects. Keeping the palette limited helped maintain a cohesive look while making the game's visuals feel distinctly retro and vaporwave-inspired.

04 / TECHNICAL DEVELOPMENT

Technical Development

The technical development focused on turning the game's core concept into a functional and responsive gameplay experience.

Working within a game jam required prioritizing the systems that had the greatest impact on the player experience. Development was centered around quickly implementing mechanics, testing them in context, and making adjustments as the game evolved.

05 / ITERATION

Iteration & Feedback

Iteration was an important part of the development process. With the limited timeframe of the jam, I had to continually evaluate which changes would have the biggest impact on the finished game.

Testing helped identify areas where gameplay, presentation, and overall clarity could be improved. These changes were incorporated throughout development rather than being left entirely until the end of the project.

06 / FINAL RESULT

Final Result

Hyperwave Drive was submitted to the Mashup Game Jam 2025 and received the following results during judging:

  • #4 — Art — 4.176
  • #9 — Overall — 3.353
  • #11 — Design — 3.059
  • #11 — Sound — 3.059
  • #12 — Fun — 3.000
  • #18 — Originality — 2.824

The project gave me experience working through a complete game development cycle under a strict deadline, from the initial concept and implementation through iteration, polish, and final submission.
