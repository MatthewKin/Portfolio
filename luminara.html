01 / OVERVIEW

Project Overview

Luminara was developed over the course of one week for Uni Jam 2025. The project was created as a game jam entry, requiring the game to be designed, developed, and polished within a limited timeframe.

The game centers around restoring a magical forest in the sky by lighting lost lanterns. The core concept combines exploration and puzzle-focused gameplay with a whimsical visual direction.

Working within the short development period required prioritizing the core gameplay experience while iterating on the game's visuals, mechanics, and presentation.

03 / VISUAL DEVELOPMENT

Visual Development

The visual development of Luminara focused on creating a magical and atmospheric environment that supported the game's theme of restoring light to a lost forest.

Lighting played an important role in setting the mood of each environment. Brighter areas helped create a feeling of warmth and wonder, while darker areas made the forest feel more mysterious and emphasized the importance of restoring the lost light.

As the player restored the lanterns, lighting became part of the visual progression of the game. The contrast between darker areas and illuminated spaces helped communicate the feeling that the forest was gradually coming back to life.

Visual elements were developed alongside the gameplay so that lighting, environments, characters, and effects could work together to establish the game's identity and atmosphere.

04 / TECHNICAL DEVELOPMENT

Technical Development

The technical development of Luminara focused on implementing the game's core gameplay systems and making sure the different mechanics worked together within the limited development time.

One of the main technical mechanics in Luminara was the constellation system. Stars could be placed throughout the level and connected together to form constellations. These constellations were used as more than just a visual element; they became part of the player's movement and platforming.

As the player placed stars and completed constellation patterns, the connected stars formed platforms that could be used to navigate through the environment. This made the constellation mechanic directly influence how the player approached each section of the level, requiring them to think about where stars should be placed in order to create a usable path.

The system connected the game's theme with its core gameplay. Instead of simply decorating the sky with stars, the player actively created the platforms they needed to progress. This allowed the constellation mechanic to function as both a puzzle system and a platforming mechanic.

Working within a week-long jam also required keeping the technical scope focused while still leaving enough time for iteration, visual development, and final polish.

05 / ITERATION

Iteration & Feedback

Iteration was an important part of the development process. Testing helped identify areas where the gameplay and presentation could be improved before the final submission.

Feedback from the game jam provided insight into how players experienced the game's mechanics, puzzles, and presentation. This helped guide the final adjustments made before submission.

06 / FINAL RESULT

Final Result

Luminara was completed as part of Uni Jam 2025 and received strong results across multiple judging categories, including #3 in Fun & Engagement, #4 Overall, #5 in Presentation, and #5 in Theme.

The project gave me the opportunity to develop a complete game within a one-week production cycle while balancing gameplay, visual development, technical implementation, and polish.
