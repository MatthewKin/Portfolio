01 / OVERVIEW

Project Overview

Deadwired was created for Pixel Game Jam 2025 and was my first-ever experience participating in a game jam. The project challenged me to take an idea from concept to a complete playable game within the limited timeframe of the jam.

The game follows L1CH, a reconstructed mind housed within a mechanized body. With only fragments of a previous life remaining, L1CH explores a fractured future world while being guided by the mysterious AI known as ARIS.

A major part of the game's concept revolves around the ReFracture Card system. These cards allow the player to augment their mechanical form, interact with the environment, and overcome obstacles while progressing through the world.

As my first game jam, Deadwired also became an introduction to working under strict development constraints. I had to balance gameplay, programming, pixel art, sound, music, and overall presentation while keeping the scope achievable.

02 / DEVELOPMENT

Development Process

01

CONCEPT

The initial concept for Deadwired centered around a reconstructed mechanical body and a character attempting to recover fragments of their forgotten identity. The idea established the game's futuristic setting and narrative direction.

02

DESIGN

The ReFracture Card system became one of the main gameplay ideas. Cards were designed around the concept of modifying the player's mechanical form and using those modifications to interact with the environment.

03

IMPLEMENTATION

The core gameplay systems were implemented while keeping the scope focused enough to remain achievable during the jam. This stage focused on turning the central mechanics into a playable experience.

04

ITERATION

As the game became playable, systems and presentation were adjusted to improve how the different mechanics worked together and to make the experience clearer for the player.

05

POLISH

The final stage focused on visual presentation, pixel art, sound effects, music, and additional details that helped give Deadwired a more complete identity.

06

SUBMISSION

Deadwired was submitted to Pixel Game Jam 2025, marking my first experience taking a game through an entire jam from initial concept to final submission.

07

RESULTS

The project received a 4.000 score in both Innovation and Overall Presentation, along with a 4.000 Pixel Art score. These results gave me useful feedback on the strengths of the project as my first game jam.

03 / VISUAL DEVELOPMENT

Visual Development

The visual development of Deadwired focused on creating a pixel-art identity that fit the game's futuristic setting. The visual style helped communicate the mechanical nature of L1CH while supporting the darker science-fiction atmosphere of the world.

Pixel art was also an important part of the game's presentation during the jam. Deadwired received a 4.000 score in the Pixel Art category during judging.

04 / TECHNICAL DEVELOPMENT

Technical Development

The technical development of Deadwired focused on turning the game's central concepts into a playable experience while working within the restrictions of a game jam.

The ReFracture Card system was an important part of the technical design. The cards were intended to give the player different ways to modify their mechanical form and interact with the environment.

Because this was my first game jam, one of the biggest technical challenges was learning how to keep the scope under control. Rather than continuing to expand the project with additional systems, development had to stay focused on the core gameplay experience.

This process gave me experience with prioritizing mechanics, integrating different systems, and making technical decisions based on the limited time available.

05 / ITERATION

Iteration & Feedback

Developing Deadwired during a game jam required continuously evaluating which ideas were worth keeping and which needed to be simplified. With limited development time, iteration was focused on maintaining the core concept while making the game achievable within the jam.

The final judging results provided feedback across several areas of the project. Deadwired received a 4.000 score for Innovation and Overall Presentation, as well as a 4.000 score for Pixel Art.

Other categories, including Sound Effects, Creativity, Effort, and Theme Relevance, provided additional areas to reflect on as I continued learning from my first experience with game jam development.

06 / FINAL RESULT

Final Result

Deadwired was completed for Pixel Game Jam 2025 and became my first-ever game jam project. The finished game combined gameplay, narrative, pixel art, sound, and visual presentation into a complete playable experience.

More importantly, the project gave me my first experience working through the entire game development process under a strict deadline. It taught me how to prioritize features, work within a limited scope, and make decisions about where development time would have the greatest impact.

The project received a 4.000 score in Innovation, Overall Presentation, and Pixel Art, along with additional judging scores across the other categories of Pixel Game Jam 2025.
