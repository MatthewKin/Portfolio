ABOUT ME

About Me

I'm Matthew Kin, a Computer Science: Game Design student at UCSC with a passion for creating engaging and interactive game experiences.

I'm especially interested in the intersection of programming and game design, focusing on gameplay systems, mechanics, and player experience. I enjoy figuring out how technical systems can be turned into interesting mechanics that make games feel dynamic and rewarding to play.

Through both academic and personal projects, I've gained experience with programming, gameplay development, and game design. I'm always looking to build on my technical skills, experiment with new ideas, and create projects that are both fun to play and interesting to explore.
